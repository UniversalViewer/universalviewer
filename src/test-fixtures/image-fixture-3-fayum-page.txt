A page made for the end-to-end tests: the same page as pdf-fixture-2-fayum.pdf
(its header reads "Universal Viewer PDF test fixture 2 / 3"), titled "Fayum
Mummy Portrait", with a short description and, as its picture, the Fayum
mummy portrait British Museum EA29772 (see image-fixture-4-fayum-portrait.txt
for the source and licence). Its footer credits the picture: "Image: Fayum
mummy portrait, British Museum EA29772. Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons.
Generated for the Universal Viewer e2e test suite - universalviewer.io".

The picture is public domain, as Wikimedia Commons gives it (it gives no
licence URI, so the manifests give these files no rights, and credit the
portrait in their requiredStatement). The page's title, text and layout were
written for this repository, and are under its MIT License (see
LICENSE.txt); the picture keeps its own licence. Rights can't give the MIT
License, so the manifests credit the pages as "made for the Universal Viewer
tests (MIT License)".
