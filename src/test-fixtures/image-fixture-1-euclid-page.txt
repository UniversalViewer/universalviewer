A page made for the end-to-end tests: the same page as pdf-fixture-1-euclid.pdf
(its header reads "Universal Viewer PDF test fixture 1 / 3"), titled "Euclid's
Elements, Book II", with a short description and, as its picture, P. Oxy. I
29 (see image-fixture-2-euclid-papyrus.txt for the source and licence). Its
footer credits the picture: "Image: P. Oxy. I 29, Penn Museum E2748. Public
domain, via Wikimedia Commons. Generated for the Universal Viewer e2e test
suite - universalviewer.io".

The picture is public domain, as Wikimedia Commons gives it (it gives no
licence URI, so the manifests give these files no rights, and credit P. Oxy. I
29 in their requiredStatement or attribution). The page's title, text and
layout were written for this repository, and are under its MIT License (see
LICENSE.txt); the picture keeps its own licence. Rights can't give the MIT
License, so the manifests credit the pages as "made for the Universal Viewer
tests (MIT License)".
