black-sand-raven.aac and black-sand-raven.m3u8

"Yellowstone sound library - Common Raven - 001": as the thermal features of
Black Sand Basin bubble in the background, a raven calls to its mate and takes
flight. Recorded by David Restivo, National Park Service, on 23 October 2013,
for the Yellowstone Sound Library (NPS and Montana State University's Acoustic
Atlas):
https://www.nps.gov/yell/learn/photosmultimedia/sounds-raven.htm
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Yellowstone_sound_library_-_Common_Raven_-_001.mp3

Licence: public domain, as a work of the United States federal government.

The original MP3 (mono, 48 kHz, 12.6 seconds; SHA-1
5aba13dc8d0efcddf4c1514e5d05b9532874ea5b, as Wikimedia Commons gives it) was
converted to AAC at 64 kbps with macOS's built-in afconvert:

  afconvert -f adts -d aac -b 64000 raven.mp3 black-sand-raven.aac

black-sand-raven.m3u8 is a hand-written HLS playlist with the AAC file as its
only segment.

Used by the end-to-end tests as a local HLS stream that plays, both in
browsers' own HLS players and with hls.js, so they don't depend on a remote
server. (stream.m3u8, whose segment is Ogg audio, is the stream that can't be
played.)
